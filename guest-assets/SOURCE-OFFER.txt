DroidLayer Guest Assets
Written Offer for Complete Corresponding Source

For every executable or object-code work covered by GNU GPL version 2 that
DroidLayer distributes with these guest assets, DroidLayer offers any third
party a complete machine-readable copy of the corresponding source code,
including the scripts and configuration used to control compilation and
installation.

This offer is valid for at least three years after the last date on which
DroidLayer distributes the applicable object code. The charge, if any, will
not exceed the physical cost of performing source distribution. A recipient
may make a request through the publisher or support channel from which the
recipient obtained DroidLayer. Include the accompanying BUILD-INPUTS file so
the exact distributed build can be identified.

The accompanying GPL-SOURCES.md also gives immediate source archive URLs,
SHA256 values, final configuration artifacts, and the build entry point.
Those download locations supplement this written offer; they do not shorten
its validity.
